# Window Openings

Status: **FIXED datums; opening implementation currently DEFERRED**

Openings are defined from the **finished floor level** for architectural dimensions. Structural coordinates are obtained by adding the floor build-up/offset between the structural datum and finished floor.

## Standard facade window

- sill level: +800 mm from finished floor
- window/opening height: 1300 mm
- top level: +2100 mm from finished floor

The +2100 mm top datum is shared with the standard exterior opening alignment.

## Current implementation stage

Window products themselves are outside V1. At the present floor-field abstraction stage, openings are intentionally postponed because they do not affect the generic floor-field geometry/layer mechanics.
