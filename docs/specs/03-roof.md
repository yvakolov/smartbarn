# Roof

Status: **FIXED where specified; detailed joints DEFERRED**

## Geometry

- roof type: gable
- roof pitch: 30° from horizontal
- gable slope: 30°
- current base configuration: no roof overhang
- roofing reference: C21 profiled sheet

## Reference plane

The roof datum is a sloped reference plane starting at the **upper outer edge/line of the longitudinal wall and its top binding (mauerlat)** and running toward the ridge at 30°.

Changing roof-layer thickness must not move this datum.

## Rafters

Current rafter section: **45 × 200 mm**.

## Lower rafter cut concept

At the longitudinal wall support the rafter geometry is defined conceptually as:

1. sloped underside of the rafter;
2. horizontal bearing seat on the wall/mauerlat;
3. vertical cut at the exterior wall plane;
4. the triangle projecting beyond that vertical plane is removed.

The future wind/fascia board fits at this roof edge.

## Construction nodes

Exact roof/wall cuts, fasteners and detailed junction geometry are to be described in a separate construction-node specification. They are intentionally not finalized in the generic roof/field model.
