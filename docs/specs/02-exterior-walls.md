# Exterior Walls

Status: **FIXED where specified**

## Reference plane

The exterior wall reference plane is the **exterior face of the wall**. Wall thickness develops inward. The exterior wall datum aligns with the edge of the floor field.

## SIP wall panel

- Total thickness: 174 mm
- Exterior skin: OSB 12 mm
- Insulation: 150 mm
- Interior skin: OSB 12 mm

## Timber frame inside SIP assembly

- lower binding: 45 × 145 mm
- top binding: 45 × 145 mm
- end binding: 45 × 145 mm
- panel connecting beam: 90 × 145 mm

The bindings and connecting beams form a spatial timber frame inside the SIP construction.

## Connecting-beam rule

The 90 × 145 mm connecting beam joins two adjacent SIP panels:

- 145 mm wide face aligns with the width/depth of the 45 × 145 mm binding system
- the 90 mm dimension lies across the panel joint
- 45 mm of the beam enters one SIP panel
- 45 mm enters the adjacent SIP panel

It is therefore a real structural panel connector, not merely a stud placed beside the joint.

## Geometry hierarchy

Longitudinal walls define the overall building width and are master geometry.

The gable/front wall is dependent geometry and is inserted **between the inner faces of the longitudinal walls**.

## Top support

The upper edge of the longitudinal SIP wall together with its embedded top binding acts as the support/mauerlat datum for the roof.
