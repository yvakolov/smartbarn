# Construction Consumables

Status: **FIXED where specified**

## SIP floor fasteners

Ring-shank drum nail:

- size: 2.5 × 50 mm
- spacing: 150 mm
- used from both OSB sides at SIP joints
- BOM norm: 14 nails per linear metre of joint

## SIP floor foam

PENOSIL GoldGun 65:

- can: 750 ml
- application: two longitudinal beads plus zigzag
- design norm: 6.0 L per linear metre of joint
- procurement norm: 6.6 L per linear metre of joint
- procurement conversion: 1 can per 9 linear metres of joint

The design and procurement norms are intentionally separate values.

## Timber protection

NEOMID 430 ECO is the fixed treatment for the timber members currently specified for the zero-floor SIP assembly.

## Other V1 consumable categories

The overall V1 envelope includes fasteners and auxiliary consumables for walls, facade and roofing, but exact types/quantities not explicitly fixed yet remain **TBD** and must not be generated as canonical values by the software.
