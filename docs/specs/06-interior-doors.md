# Interior Doors

Status: **FIXED dimensional standard; partitions/products currently DEFERRED**

Reference product family: Porta Prima.

## Standard door leaves and rough openings

| Door leaf, mm | Rough opening, mm |
| --- | --- |
| 600 × 2000 | 680 × 2050 |
| 700 × 2000 | 780 × 2050 |
| 800 × 2000 | 880 × 2050 |
| 900 × 2000 | 980 × 2050 |

Opening height is referenced from the **finished floor level**.

Interior partitions and purchased door products are outside the current V1 scope, but these dimensions are the canonical Smart Barn opening standard when the partition/opening module is introduced.
